\begin{frame}{The wedge}
  \adminikicker{go to market}
  \vspace{2mm}

  \adminilead{We would validate the compliance wedge first---and say plainly
    that it is not a tested market yet.}

  \vspace{3mm}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Design partners:} newsrooms and schools, where traceability
          already matters.
    \item \textbf{Distribution:} a free trial converts on the first video they
          check.
    \item \textbf{Moat:} verified citations and an audit log competitors do not
          produce.
    \item \textbf{Retention:} the audit log is what a subscription renews on.
  \end{itemize}
  \note{%
    The honest position: the subscription spreads through a free trial, but the
    buyer we would validate first is the institution that already has to show
    its work. Newsrooms and schools are design partners, because traceability
    matters to them today. The moat is the same thing that earns idea and
    innovation: every verdict points at a line on a page we confirmed contains
    it, and the check log is auditable---which is also what a subscription
    renews on. Say plainly that this is the wedge we would validate first, not a
    tested business.%
  }
\end{frame}
