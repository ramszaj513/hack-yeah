\begin{frame}{Why this is Defence}
  \adminikicker{relation to category}
  \vspace{2mm}

  \adminilead{Disinformation is not a content problem. It is a defence problem,
    because the target is a population rather than a system.}

  \vspace{3mm}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Defence means protecting a population's picture of reality.
    \item The attack surface is the moment a claim is believed.
    \item The tool has to sit where the belief forms: the player.
  \end{itemize}
