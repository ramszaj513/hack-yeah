\begin{frame}{We are not first---here is what we add}
  \adminikicker{prior art}
  \vspace{2mm}

  \adminilead{PopUpFactCheck, Facty, DeepVerify and Live Fact Checker already do
    in-video fact-checking. These are the differences we add.}

  \vspace{3mm}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Citation verification:} the quoted line is confirmed to be on
          the cited page before it is shown.
    \item \textbf{Selected text:} check a passage you highlight, anywhere---
          not only inside a video.
    \item \textbf{Nature and tone:} a second pass names how the speech argues,
          beside whether its facts are right.
  \end{itemize}
  \note{%
    We are not first, and a judge may know it. Get in front of it here rather
    than being corrected. The narrow claim is defensible: none of the
    alternatives verify that a cited page contains the quoted line. What we add
    on top is checking selected text anywhere---a passage the viewer highlights
    on any page---and a second pass that reads the nature and tone of the
    speech, naming how it argues rather than only whether its facts are right.
    Never claim first or only.%
  }
\end{frame}
