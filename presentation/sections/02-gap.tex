\begin{frame}{The correction arrives too late}
  \adminikicker{the gap}
  \vspace{2mm}

  \adminilead{Someone watches a twenty-minute video tonight.}

  \vspace{2mm}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item If one claim in it is false, the correction---if it ever comes---
          lands about two weeks from now.
    \item Nine times out of ten it never lands at all.
    \item By then they have already repeated it at work.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1mm}

  \adminicallout[adminilink]{We move the check to the moment of exposure.}

  \adminisource{Community Notes: 14-day median, 90\%+ never published
    \textperiodcentered\ Harvard Misinformation Review, 2025 (external).}
  \note{%
    Tell it as a person, not a statistic: someone watches a twenty-minute
    video tonight; if one claim is false, the correction---if it ever
    comes---lands about two weeks from now, and nine times out of ten it never
    lands at all. By then they have already repeated it at work. Then point at
    the callout: we move the check to the moment of exposure.%
  }
\end{frame}
